Each player uses their own phone. Both need internet: the same Wi-Fi, mobile data, or one phone sharing its hotspot with the other.

How to play
  • Move: drag your thumb around the blue court map. It's a top-down view of your half: left and right move you toward or away from the net, up and down move you across the court. Get in line with the ball yourself.
  • Hit: tap Dink (soft, lands in the kitchen), Drive (fast and low) or Lob (high and deep) as the ball reaches your paddle. Hit early to go crosscourt, late to go down the line.
  • Serve: tap any shot. The serve must land in the yellow diagonal box: past the kitchen, inside the baseline and sideline.
  • Two-bounce rule: the return of serve and the third shot must bounce before anyone hits them.
  • Kitchen: no volleys (hitting before the bounce) while standing in the 7-foot zone by the net.
  • Scoring: only the serving side scores. First to 11, win by 2. In doubles both partners serve before a side out, and the game starts at 0–0–2.
  • Reading the ball: the dashed ring shows where an incoming ball will land. Every bounce leaves a green IN or red OUT mark. A green ring around the ball means you can hit it; red means let it bounce.
  • Friend online: one phone taps Host game and reads out the 4-letter code; the other taps Join game and types it in. The host picks singles or doubles and starts.
  • Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move, Z X C (or J K L) to hit. P opens the menu.
